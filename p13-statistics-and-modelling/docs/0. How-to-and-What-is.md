
Dataset Used: 

A fictional wealth-management and brokerage business, with data covering:
- Client demographics, wealth tiers, and risk profiles
- Digital adoption and Customer Digitalization Index (CDI)
- Brokerage orders, execution latency, and trading activity
- Portfolio returns, investment strategies, and market benchmarks
- Account onboarding, advisory adoption, and complaints
- Daily market returns and trading-volume time series

Python Libraries used:

Library	and Primary purpose
scipy.stats	
- t-tests, ANOVA, variance, correlation, non-parametric and distribution tests

statsmodels	
- Regression inference, proportion tests, ADF, KPSS, autocorrelation tests

pandas	
- Loading, filtering, joining, and preparing datasets

numpy
- Numerical calculations and simulations

matplotlib
- Histograms, boxplots, scatterplots, time-series charts

seaborn
- Higher-level statistical visualizations, optional
